2. Principle

MORNING MUG works without an account, without sign-in and without entering personal data. All puzzles are included in the game; what you type into the grid never leaves the device. Game progress (started and solved puzzles, times, streak, rest days, mugs, statistics per language), settings (sounds, music, vibration, large print, high contrast, language), the "Ad-free" purchase status, purchased hints and the bonus puzzle book are stored only locally on your device and are not sent to us. We run no server of our own, receive no personal data from you and use no analytics or crash-reporting services. "Options → Reset progress" deletes your progress; uninstalling removes all local game data.

3. Google Play

Downloads, updates and purchases are handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive aggregated, non-personal statistics from Google (e.g. install numbers, anonymous crash statistics).

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers optional purchases via Google Play Billing: "Ad-free" (one-time), a starter pack (one-time; 50 hints and a bonus book of 40 puzzles) and hint packs (15, 35 or 100 hints, can be bought repeatedly). Payment data is received only by Google, not by us. The game only receives the purchase confirmation (product ID, purchase token) to unlock the purchase and stores "purchased yes/no" and the hint stock locally. "Options → Restore purchases" queries the one-time purchases again. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. There are no subscriptions and no loot boxes. Today's puzzle and the puzzles of the last seven days are always free; older archive puzzles can be unlocked with a rewarded video or with "Ad-free". Hints do not change puzzles or scoring.

The game is free and financed by advertising. There are (a) ad banners at the bottom edge of menus and below the puzzle, which never cover the grid, the keyboard or buttons and do not appear in the tutorial or at game start, (b) optional rewarded videos that you start yourself (e.g. two hints per video or an older archive puzzle), (c) occasional full-screen interstitial ads only after a solved puzzle – not in the first puzzles, at least one minute apart and never during a puzzle – and (d) at most once a day an ad when you return to the game (at least four hours apart, not on first launch). We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, address as above). After buying "Ad-free" you will see no ads.

You can change your choice at any time under "Options → Privacy settings"; withdrawing consent there is as easy as giving it and brings the notice back.
