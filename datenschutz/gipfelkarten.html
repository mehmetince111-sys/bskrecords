GIPFELKARTEN works without an account, without sign-in and without entering personal data. Game progress (levels, stars, postcard album, daily puzzle streak), settings (e.g. language) and the "Ad-free" purchase status are stored only locally on your device and are not sent to us. Downloads, updates and purchases are handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive aggregated, non-personal statistics from Google (e.g. install numbers, anonymous crash statistics).

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Real-money purchases are optional: the one-time purchase "Ad-free" (removes all ads; the extra cards after a lost round then come without a video), a one-time starter pack and three helper packs (jokers, undos, +5 cards; can be bought repeatedly). Every level can be finished without buying. Announced further content ("soon") cannot be bought at the moment. Purchases are handled via Google Play Billing. Payment data is received only by Google, not by us. The game only receives the purchase confirmation (product ID, purchase token) to unlock the purchase and stores "purchased yes/no" and the credited helpers locally. "Restore" in the shop or in the options checks the purchase again after reinstalling. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. There are no subscriptions, no loot boxes and no purchasable in-game currency.

GIPFELKARTEN shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, address as above): banners only in the menus (title, trip map, album, daily puzzle – never above the playing field), an occasional interstitial ad after a completed level (never in the first levels, never after a loss, with a minimum interval) and – optionally – a short rewarded video after a lost round (+5 cards, at most 5 times a day). With "Ad-free" no ads are loaded.

  • Consent: where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) we ask for your consent via Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are loaded for the first time; you can change your choice at any time under "Options → Privacy settings". Without consent, at most non-personalised or limited ads are loaded.

  • Purpose: serving, measuring and, where consented, personalising ads; fraud prevention.
  • Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG; for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Withdrawal: at any time in the game under "Options → Privacy settings". You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.
  • Transfers to third countries (in particular the USA): Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses apply in addition.
  • More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Without an internet connection no ads are loaded; the game itself works fully offline.

Internet and network state (for ads and purchases), advertising ID (AD_ID), Google Play Billing (BILLING) and vibration (can be switched off). No permissions for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.
